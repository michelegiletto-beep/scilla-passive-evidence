{\LARGE\textbf{SCILLA PASSIVE}}\\{\large Industrial Evidence Brief - Public Evidence Release 1.0.0}\\[4pt]
\textbf{Hypothesis.} Use mechanically scanning merchant radars as non-cooperative, moving illumination opportunities for a track that already exists. The product hypothesis is a passive \emph{second-observation} layer, not a replacement radar.

\textbf{Evidence status.} Simulation/public research only. No measured RF performance.\\\textbf{DOI.} \href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22229086}{10.5281/zenodo.22229086}.

\textbf{Hard result.} Passive updates beat propagation-only in 94.4\% of 54 physics stress cells. Three failure cells are preserved.

\textbf{Hard negative result.} The EIG policy does not robustly beat shortest-pulse selection; it wins 49.7\% of paired nominal worlds. \textbf{Optimizer moat not demonstrated.}

\textbf{What remains unproven.} Sea-clutter suppression, direct-path dynamic range, emitter association, real bistatic RCS, field geolocation, customer economics and operational utility.

\textbf{Why an industrial partner matters.} A radar/UAS/surveillance organization already possessing RF labs or archived mission data can close the dominant evidence gap at low marginal cost through independent replay or partner-sponsored receive-only validation.

\textbf{Diligence object.} Frozen equations + simulator + 300-world paired benchmark + 54-cell physics grid + 27-cell integrity grid + preserved failure history + source ledger + reproduction protocol.

\textbf{Next external gate.} Independent reproduction / customer replay / partner-sponsored RF validation.

\textit{No traction, acquisition interest, patentability or field performance is asserted.}
