# Industrial Diligence Brief

## Decision in one paragraph

SCILLA PASSIVE is currently a simulation-supported measurement-architecture hypothesis: an externally cued maritime track may benefit from a valid opportunistic bistatic second observation generated by non-cooperative merchant-radar illumination. The published evidence does **not** establish an optimizer moat, real RF detectability, operational range, product readiness, or customer value. The rational next investment is independent reproduction and partner-sponsored replay/measurement—not another internally selected optimizer.

## Material scientific caveat

Post-publication QA identified partition-dependent process-noise behavior in `1.0.0`. A candidate event can split propagation time, and a rejected update can therefore affect legacy covariance evolution. The release has been preserved rather than silently rewritten. `1.1.0-candidate` contains a correction for falsification, but it is not a released result and cannot yet support a new performance claim.

This caveat is a diligence strength only if the boundary remains explicit. It becomes a kill condition if candidate numbers are mixed with the DOI evidence or the defect is hidden.

## Strongest current claim

> Under the declared simulated regimes, a valid passive second observation often improves an uncertain track relative to propagation alone; sophisticated donor selection does not robustly outperform a transparent simple baseline.

## Product hypothesis—not product claim

The plausible integration point is a cue-driven evidence layer inside an existing surveillance workflow. It could rank or ingest episodic bistatic opportunities when active dwell, electro-optical pointing, bandwidth, emissions, or operator attention are constrained. This remains a proposed integration architecture until real interfaces and measurements exist.

## Diligence conclusion

**Advance only to external reproduction/replay.** Do not fund a proprietary donor optimizer, claim operational passive radar, or promote `1.1.0-candidate` until the corresponding gates pass. The current asset is the transparent evidence corpus, negative result, reproducible architecture model, and defined falsification path.
