# Claim Hierarchy

## Publicly defensible now
**C1 - SIMULATED:** Under the declared model, valid opportunistic bistatic second observations materially improve many externally cued tracks relative to propagation without passive updates.

**C2 - SIMULATED:** The benefit has strong regime dependence. Donor density, clutter and bistatic RCS can eliminate it.

**C3 - NEGATIVE RESULT:** A sophisticated donor-selection moat is not established. Simple pulse-mode selection is highly competitive.

## Not defensible now
- measured target detection performance;
- field geolocation accuracy;
- guaranteed useful range;
- non-AIS vessel detection;
- operational maritime surveillance effectiveness;
- AI superiority;
- quantum advantage;
- patentability or freedom to operate;
- customer traction or acquisition interest.

## Publication rule
Every quantitative performance sentence must contain an evidence tag or context making clear whether it is SIMULATED, DERIVED, PUBLICLY SOURCED or MEASURED.
