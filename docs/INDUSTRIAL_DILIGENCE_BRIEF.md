# Industrial Diligence / Acquisition Readiness Brief

## What an industrial reviewer can verify without contacting the author
- equations and assumptions;
- executable simulation;
- frozen seeds/configuration;
- paired baselines;
- confidence intervals;
- failed versions;
- failure regimes;
- source/prior-art ledger;
- reproducibility protocol;
- clear evidence taxonomy.

## What still requires the author / a partner
- interpretation of integration architecture;
- adaptation to a buyer's real sensor/data formats;
- RF bench and field calibration plan;
- customer-specific decision-cost model;
- continuing unpublished implementation work.

## Acquisition-readiness scorecard (proposed management assessment)
Scientific credibility: 8/10
Reproducibility: 9/10
Public discoverability before publication: 3/10
Integration definition: 7/10
Measured physical evidence: 0/10
External validation: 0/10
Customer traction: 0/10
Capital efficiency: 9/10

The next largest value increments are therefore public discoverability and external validation, not another internal optimizer.
